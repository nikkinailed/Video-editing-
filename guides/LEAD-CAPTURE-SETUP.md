# The Capture: comment ➝ auto DM ➝ short form ➝ your database

Goal from the conference slide: every keyword comment ends up as a contact (name, phone, email) in Nikki's own database, tagged by what they asked for.

Built on GoHighLevel (the platform behind nikkinail.com), which has Instagram comment automations, forms and a contact database in one place. Nothing here goes live without Nikki's OK.

## 1. One form per keyword (GoHighLevel ➝ Sites ➝ Forms)
Fields on every form: First name, Phone, Email (all required), plus one question:
"What are you thinking about?" More space / Less upkeep / More land / Just exploring
Add the consent line under the button: "By entering you agree to get texts and emails from Nikki Nail, Shelley Home Group of Cummings & Co Realtors. Reply STOP to opt out."

| Keyword | Form title | Button | After submit, show | Contact tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NEXT | Get your Next Home Guide | Send my guide | The Next Home Guide link | kw-next, rightsizing |
| WIN | Enter to win the {place} gift card | Enter me | "You're in! Winner drawn {day}." plus the LOCAL guide link | kw-win, giveaway-{place} |
| LOCAL | Get your Carroll County local guide | Send my guide | Local guide link | kw-local |
| MOVING | Get your move up guide | Send my guide | Move up guide link | kw-moving |
| HOME | Get your home buyer guide | Send my guide | Buyer guide link | kw-home |
| DEAL | Get the Deal of the Week details | Send the details | Listing link | kw-deal |
| FALL / WINTER / SPRING / SUMMER | Get your {season} guide | Send my guide | Season guide link | kw-{season} |

Put each form on its own simple page: nikkinail.com/next, /win, /local, /moving, /home, /deal, /fall. Short links read well in a DM.

## 2. Instagram comment automation (GoHighLevel ➝ Automation ➝ Workflows)
Trigger: Instagram comment contains keyword (one workflow per keyword, all posts).
Actions:
1. Reply to the comment: "Sent it to your DMs! 💜"
2. Send Instagram DM: "Hi {first name}! 🏡 Here's your link: nikkinail.com/{keyword}. Pop in your name, phone and email and it's yours."
3. Wait 24 hours. If the form isn't submitted, DM: "Just checking you got the link 💜 nikkinail.com/{keyword}"

## 3. After the form (same workflow)
1. Create or update the contact, add the tags above.
2. Email the guide link right away (subject "Your {guide} is here 🏡"). Links only, never attachments.
3. NEXT only: start the 4 email follow up in guides/next-dm-auto-reply.md.
4. WIN only: add to the "giveaway-{place}" list for the random draw.
5. Notify Nikki by text for anyone who picks "More space" or "More land".

## 4. Check it weekly
Contacts ➝ filter by tag shows who came from which reel. Grok Bot posts the weekly count per keyword on the crosswalk board.
