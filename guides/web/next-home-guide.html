Step 2

Which move fits you?

Rightsizing isn't always smaller. It's the home that fits this season of your family.

Move up

More room for a growing family

  • Bedrooms for everyone
  • A mudroom for the sports gear
  • A yard the kids and dog can use

Move down

Less upkeep, more life

  • Main level living
  • Little or no mowing
  • Lock and leave for tournament weekends

Move out

More land, maybe less house

  • Acreage over square footage
  • A flat yard for practice
  • Room for animals or a shop

Step 3

Buy first or sell first?

Sell first

You know exactly what you can spend. Ask for extra time or a short rent back so you only move once.

Buy first

Move on your schedule. Your lender shows you if carrying both homes for a short time or using your equity works.

Home sale contingency

Your offer depends on your home selling. It works best when your home is priced and prepped to go fast.

Time both together

Both settlements on the same day or week, so you go straight from one home to the next.

Your lender and I will map the option that fits your numbers and your calendar, travel sports weekends included.
