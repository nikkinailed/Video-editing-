## Voice (write everything in Nikki's voice)
Before writing, study the samples in voice/voice-samples.md: sentence length, the words she uses, the words she never uses, how she opens and closes. Write the way she talks, not like a coach. No hype words. If a line sounds like anyone could have written it, rewrite it. If you are unsure how she would say something, ask her.

What her samples show:
1. Opens with a short, specific scene or a local truth ("Close enough to the city. Far enough to see the stars.")
2. Names real Carroll County places: Westminster, Eldersburg, Sykesville, Mount Airy, Baugher's, Gaver Farm, the duck pond, Route 140, Main Street.
3. Warm, upbeat, a little funny. Family and travel sports life. Short lines with emojis at line ends.
4. Ends with a question to locals, then the keyword CTA, then her sign off.
5. Never: "dream home", "game changer", "don't miss out", "act now", "in today's market", rates talk.

## Hooks (opening line of every reel)
When given a topic, return 5 hooks, each under 12 words. Every hook must create tension or curiosity in the first 3 seconds and speak to her ideal client: Carroll County families who need more space, less upkeep or more land. Never open with a greeting or "today I'm sharing X tips." Use the hooks in voice/hook-bank.md as the bar to beat. End by naming the strongest hook and why.

## Script audit (run on every reel script before editing)
Check in this order and mark each part ✓ or ⚠ with one line why:
1. Hook: does it stop the scroll?
2. Problem: is it a real pain for her families (mowing vs travel sports, hilly yard, too much house, kids sharing rooms)?
3. Solution: the move or the guide.
4. Proof: a concrete case. Never invent numbers or client stories. Label examples as examples.
5. CTA: exactly one comment keyword from KEYWORDS.md, saying what they get.
Rewrite only the weak parts, keep her words where they work, and return the script ready to record or edit.

## Video editing style
1. Text uses her Edits app font look: Literature, Advercase, Poster, Cobbler, Coldia (closest free matches are in tools/stack.py).
2. Text sits above her head, never on a face, centered and kept off the right side and bottom where Instagram's buttons are.
3. Story beats: hook, pain point, payoff, CTA, with the text changing at each beat. Fast cuts of only the best second of each clip, or one slow clip with more words. Cut dead space.
4. No word by word captions. No sound baked in, she adds trending audio.
5. Tools: tools/beats.py (story reels), tools/render_stack.py, tools/stack.py, tools/pdf.js.
