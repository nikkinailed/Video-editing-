# Nikki's comment keywords

Always use these. Every on screen CTA and caption says what they get.

| Keyword | Sends | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| NEXT | Next Chapter rightsizing guide (guides/Next-Chapter-Guide-Nikki-Nail.pdf). NEXT means "your next home." | Every rightsizing reel or post: moving up, moving down, more land, less upkeep |
| MOVING | Move up guide | Relocation and move up content |
| LOCAL | Carroll County local guide | Local pride, things to do, community reels |
| HOME | Home buyer guide | Buyer content |
| DEAL | Deal of the Week | Deal of the Week post only |
| FALL, WINTER, SPRING, SUMMER | Matching seasonal guide | Seasonal local content |

Retired, do not use: MOVE UP, BUYER, SELL, LAND, GRANT, ROUND.
