# Local Spot Giveaways: one post, real contact info

Every local place Nikki features gets a gift card giveaway. People comment WIN to enter, the DM collects their email, and the business gets a shoutout (and usually a reshare).

## How it works
1. **Pick the spot.** A place Nikki actually loves (restaurant, coffee shop, farm, brewery, shop).
2. **Buy the gift card yourself** ($25 to $50). Optional: ask the owner to match it or donate one in exchange for the shoutout.
3. **Film 6 to 10 short clips** (2 to 5 seconds each): the outside and sign, the line or the crowd, the food or product up close, the owner if they're willing, and Nikki smiling there. Vertical.
4. **Send the clips plus 3 things locals love about it.** Claude fills giveaways/<place>.json and renders the reel with tools/giveaway.py within minutes.
5. **Post Monday or Tuesday, draw the winner Sunday night,** announce the winner in stories Monday, and tag the business every time.

## Hooks (pick one per reel, rotate them)
1. "Okay, I got some bad news. There's only a few weeks left to try this..." (seasonal spots, limited menus)
2. "Why is there a line outside this little spot in [town]?" (busy restaurants, farm stands)
3. "Stop right here for a chance to win..." (caption hook for every giveaway)

The first second decides the next thirty.

## Keyword: WIN
